Renderlight Privacy Policy

App: Renderlight: Live Wallpapers (com.digiera.livewallpapers)
Developer: Digiera
Effective date: 9 November 2026

In short: Renderlight does not ask you to create an account, does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location, and does not send your wallpaper choices to us. The only data that leaves your phone is handled by Google, to show ads (Google AdMob) and to process the optional Premium purchase (Google Play).

1. Who we are

Renderlight is an Android app that provides animated live wallpapers. It is published by Digiera ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL@example.com.

2. Information the app stores on your device

To work, the app saves a few settings on your device only. We never receive them.

  • Which wallpaper you picked for the home screen and the lock screen
  • Your favourites and your settings (touch and tilt, 60 FPS option, low power mode)
  • Whether Premium is unlocked on this device
  • Simple speed measurements the app takes once on your phone to choose a smooth frame rate

This data stays on your device and is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, calendar, microphone or camera data, or your precise location. The app plays no audio and has no accounts, social features or user-generated content. We run no servers that receive your personal data, and we use no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Renderlight is free. You can apply a free wallpaper by watching a short rewarded ad. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When an ad is loaded or shown, Google and its partners may collect and use:

  • Your advertising ID and device identifiers
  • Your IP address and an approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, app version)
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data (for example whether an ad loaded, was viewed or clicked)

This is used to deliver, measure and personalise ads, and to prevent fraud and abuse. We do not receive this data from Google in a form that identifies you.

Your choices.

  • Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions) the app shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can change your choice later where the form is offered again.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your phone's settings: Settings, Privacy (or Google), Ads.
  • You can avoid ads completely by buying Premium (see below).

Learn more: How Google uses data from ads and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. In-app purchase (Google Play)

Premium is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment and shares with us only whether the purchase is valid. We never see or store your card or payment details. Purchases are governed by the Google Play Terms of Service. To restore Premium on a new phone, sign in with the same Google account and open the app; it checks Google Play for your purchase.

7. Children

Renderlight is not directed to children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will help.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only parties that receive data because of your use of the app are Google (for ads, through AdMob, and for payments, through Google Play), as described above, and only as needed to provide those services.

9. Security

Data sent between the app and Google's services is encrypted in transit. Settings stored on your device are protected by Android's app sandbox.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe and the UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to or withdraw consent for ads personalisation. Because we do not hold personal data about you, the data in question sits with Google. You can manage it at your Google Account and in your phone's ads settings. You can also contact us with any request and we will answer within a reasonable time.

11. Data retention and deletion

Settings stored on your device stay until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. We keep no user data on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data held by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

12. International use

Google may process ad-related data in countries other than your own. Google's safeguards for such transfers are described in its privacy policy.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a feature. The effective date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes on Google Play.
